## Part 2 – 50 new ideas for Fellesskap

Status 2026-11-15: all built (0108) except 25 Hjertestarter and 27 the waste calendar, left out on request.

### The neighbourhood at a glance
1. **«Denne uka i nabolaget»** card at the top: how many jobs were done, new oppslag and arrangementer this week.
2. **Neighbour pulse per kommune:** «Naboer i Lillehammer hjalp hverandre 23 ganger i november». It shows the place is alive, even for new users.
3. **Weekly digest push on Sunday evening:** «Nytt i nabolaget denne uka», one push instead of many.
4. **Your own Fellesskap summary:** «Du har lånt ut 3 ting og vært på 2 arrangementer i år».
5. **One search across Fellesskap:** oppslag, arrangementer, lag and partnere in one field.
6. **Filter by nabolag** when you're a member of more than one.
7. **«Ukas bilde»:** neighbours send in a photo from the area (sunset over Mjøsa), the most-liked one becomes the page header. Moderated.

### Welcoming people
8. **«Nye naboer»** row: people who joined your nabolag this month (only if they say yes), with a «Si hei» button.
9. **A short introduction** when you join: «Hvem er du?» in one sentence, shown once in the nabolag.
10. **Nabolagsverter:** volunteers with a badge who welcome new members and can pin oppslag.
11. **Pinned oppslag** from verter at the top of the nabolag.
12. **House rules per nabolag**, written by the verter («Hold det vennlig, ingen reklame»).
13. **«Ny i Norge» språkvenn:** pair new residents with a neighbour who wants to practise Norwegian over coffee.
14. **Youth corner:** a «For ungdom» section with youth-friendly arrangementer and oppdrag that are open to youth.

### Lag, foreninger and partners
15. **Directory of local lag og foreninger** (idrettslag, velforening, korps) with a link and their next arrangement.
16. **Verified organisation accounts:** a lag can post arrangementer under its own name and logo.
17. **«Stem på neste støtte»:** neighbours vote which local lag gets the next donation round.
18. **Progress to the next payout:** «Neste utbetaling til lag og foreninger: 3 200 av 5 000 kr». This is partner money for lag, not payment between neighbours.
19. **Stories from the lag:** a photo and three sentences on what the money did («Nye drakter til G12»).
20. **Partner offers for everyone,** not only helpers: «Nabotilbud» from local shops.
21. **Partner arrangementer:** a course or open day from a partner in the calendar, with a partner badge.
22. **Follow a partner** and get a push when they post an offer or arrangement.
23. **«Spør fagfolk»:** once a month a partner (an elektriker, a rørlegger) answers neighbours' questions in a thread.
24. **«Tips en bedrift»:** neighbours suggest a local business that should become a partner, which gives you sales leads.

### Practical things in the area
25. **Hjertestarter i nærheten** from the national register, shown as public places on the map.
26. **Useful numbers per kommune:** legevakt, kommunen, renovasjon, vakttelefon.
27. **Waste collection calendar** from the kommune or the waste company, with an optional reminder the evening before.
28. **Weather warnings for the kommune** (MET) with «Trenger noen hjelp?», which leads to snømåking or other storm help.
29. **Outage board:** «Strømmen er borte på Nordseter», where others tap «Hos meg også». It closes by itself after a few hours.
30. **«Jeg kan hjelpe i dag»** during a storm or snowfall: a short list of neighbours who are free today.
31. **Vennetjeneste:** an older person can opt in to a neighbour checking in during cold weather.
32. **Seasonal checklist** («Høstsjekk for huset»), where each task can become an oppdrag with one tap.

### New kinds of oppslag
33. **«Ønsker å låne»:** you post what you need, and neighbours who own one get a push.
34. **«Byttes»:** swap things instead of giving them away.
35. **«Overskuddsmat»:** leftover food that closes itself after 24 hours.
36. **«Hagens overskudd»:** plums, apples and rhubarb in season.
37. **«Savnet dyr»:** louder than lost and found, with a push to the whole nabolag and an «Sett den?» button.
38. **«Samkjøring»:** «Kjører til Hamar lørdag, noen som vil sitte på?», with the payment note.
39. **«Anbefalinger»:** «Beste frisør i Moelv?» with answers sorted by likes.

### Arrangementer
40. **«Hvem tar med hva»** list on an arrangement (kake, grill, stoler).
41. **Repeating arrangementer** («Strikkekafé hver torsdag»).
42. **Add to the phone's calendar** with one tap (.ics).
43. **Photo album after the arrangement** that everyone who came can add to.
44. **Volunteer slots** on an arrangement: «Trenger 3 til rigging, 2 til kafé».
45. **Dugnad equipment from Utlån:** the dugnad shows which rakes and trailers neighbours lend out.
46. **Share an arrangement as an image card** for Facebook and Instagram, like the oppdrag cards.

### Neighbourhood projects
47. **Neighbourhood project with progress:** «Ny benk ved lekeplassen», with supporters and steps, no money.
48. **«Forslag til kommunen»:** collect support from neighbours and send it to the kommune as a PDF.
49. **Community badges:** «Første utlån», «5 dugnader», «Arrangør», shown on the profile.
50. **Thank-you wall:** short public thanks to a neighbour («Takk til Jonas som bar sofaen!»). Moderated, and the person must say yes before it's shown.

## Part 3 – 50 new ideas for Innboks

Status 2026-11-15: all built (0109). 34 «Pin a message» already existed.

### What needs me now
1. **«Venter på svar fra deg»** section: conversations where the other person wrote last more than 24 hours ago.
2. **«Venter på dem»:** you wrote last and nobody has answered. It shows when to nudge.
3. **Action tray at the top:** tilbud to answer, endringer to approve, vurderinger to give.
4. **The avtale's status on the row:** «Avtale lør 14:00», «Tilbud venter», «Fullført – gi vurdering».
5. **«I morgen»** strip: «Plenklipping hos Kari kl 10» above the conversations.
6. **Status filters** «Avtalt» and «Fullført» beside today's filters.

### Order
7. **Pin a conversation** to the top.
8. **Snooze:** «Minn meg på i morgen», and the conversation comes back as unread.
9. **Group the chats for one oppdrag:** four bidders on one oppdrag become one stacked row that opens into four.
10. **«Sammenlign tilbud»** straight from that group.
11. **Select several** with a long-press, then archive, mark as read or mute them all at once.
12. **Auto-archive** conversations on closed oppdrag after 30 days, with a setting to turn it off.
13. **Mute for a set time:** 1 hour, until tomorrow, or always.
14. **«Forespørsler» folder:** the first message from someone you've never dealt with lands here, so strangers don't fill the inbox.
15. **«Mistenkelig» folder:** conversations the scam warning flagged, with an explanation.

### Search
16. **Search filters:** by person, by oppdrag, «har bilder», by date.
17. **Highlight the word you searched for** in the result, and jump straight to that message.
18. **Search in voice message transcripts** too.

### Answering faster
19. **Quick reply from the row:** swipe right, write, send, without opening the chat.
20. **Your own quick replies** for all chats («Passer fint! Ses da.»), not only for tilbud.
21. **Suggested replies** under the last message («Passer fint», «Når passer det?»). Simple rules, no AI.
22. **Draft on the row:** «Utkast: Hei, jeg kan komme…» when you left text unsent, and the text stays when you come back.
23. **Undo send** for 5 seconds.
24. **Edit a sent message** for 15 minutes, marked «redigert».
25. **Schedule a message:** «Send i morgen kl 08», so you don't wake people.
26. **Away mode:** an automatic reply while you're on holiday, and your «Tilbyr hjelp» posts are paused.
27. **Response time on your profile** («Svarer vanligvis innen 1 time»), with a tip in Innboks on how to keep it.

### Notifications
28. **Quiet hours:** no pushes between 22 and 07; they arrive in the morning.
29. **Bundled pushes:** «Kari sendte 4 meldinger», not four pushes.
30. **Separate badges** for tilbud and ordinary messages on the tab.
31. **«skriver …»** on the row, not only inside the chat.
32. **Hide when I'm active,** a privacy setting for «aktiv nå».
33. **Turn read receipts off** (then you don't see others' either).

### Inside the conversation
34. **Pin a message** at the top of the chat («Portkode 1234», «Husk stige»).
35. **Shared checklist** in the chat for the job (things to buy, steps).
36. **«Del adresse» card** that can only be sent after an avtale, can be withdrawn, and hides itself after the job.
37. **«Om samtalen» page:** the oppdrag, the avtale, all photos, voice messages and files in one place.
38. **Send an oppdrag card** into a chat («Kanskje dette passer for deg?»).
39. **Short video** (up to 15 seconds), for showing what's broken.
40. **Attach a PDF** (for example a quote from a business).
41. **Edit a photo before sending:** crop it, and blur it with the tool from Legg ut.
42. **Play voice messages at 1.5× and 2×,** and drag along the waveform.
43. **Read a message aloud,** for poor eyesight or busy hands.
44. **Always translate** from a chosen language, not one message at a time.
45. **Export a conversation as a PDF** for your own records.

### Safety and youth
46. **Safety check after the first message from a stranger:** «Føles samtalen trygg?», with Ja / Rapporter.
47. **Calm banner for youth:** «Du snakker med en voksen – hold samtalen i appen og si fra hvis noe føles rart».
48. **Block and report straight from the row** with a swipe.

### After the job
49. **«Spør igjen»:** in a finished conversation, ask the same helper for the same job with one tap.
50. **Two panes on tablet and web:** the list on the left and the chat on the right.
